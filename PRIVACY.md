# Privacy Policy

Tipiṭaka.app (the website tipitaka.app and the Tipiṭaka.app apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android) is
published by the Path Nirvana Foundation. Effective 18 October 2026.

## The apps
- The apps collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- The texts, dictionaries and search run on your device. What you read, search or look up is not sent anywhere.
- Your settings, bookmarks and reading history are stored only on your device.
- The map of places can show map images from OpenStreetMap or EOX (Sentinel-2) when you choose an online map.
  Your device then requests those images from their servers, which see your IP address as with any website.
  The built-in map needs no connection.
- Links to other websites open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

## The website
- The website works like the apps, but the texts are served by our server. The server keeps standard access logs
  (IP address, page address, time, browser) for security and troubleshooting. They are not shared or sold and
  are deleted regularly.
- On the first visit the website asks ipinfo.io for the country of your IP address, to choose the script and
  language of your country. The result is not stored on our server. Choosing a script or language yourself
  avoids this.
- The website sets no cookies; settings and bookmarks are stored in your browser.

## Children
Tipiṭaka.app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
