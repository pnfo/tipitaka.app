## Getting started {#start}

- **Find a text:** tap :menu: **Contents** at the top left, or pick a book on the [home page](/).
- **Meaning of a word:** tap any Pāḷi word in the text. The dictionary opens beside the text (at the bottom on phones).
- **More options for a paragraph:** the :ellipsis: button next to each paragraph has the commentary, bookmarks, links and copying.
- **Search:** type in the box at the top, in any script.
- **Menu:** :ellipsis-vertical: at the top right has the other pages, this help and the settings.

## The menu :ellipsis-vertical: {#menu}

The :ellipsis-vertical: button at the top right opens the menu:

- The pages: :house: **Home**, :bookmark: **Bookmarks**, :users: **Places and people**, :map: **Map** and the :arrow-left-right: **Pali Script Converter**.
- The settings used most: **Pali script**, **Interface language**, the colours (**Appearance**) and the **Text size**. :settings: **More settings** has all the others.
- :circle-question-mark: **Help** and :info: **About**.

On a wide screen the menu stays open at the right, like the contents at the left. :x: closes it; when you open it again, :pin: **Keep open** keeps it there. While the dictionary is open, the menu makes room for it.

The pages you open from the menu have :x: at the top: it takes you back to where you were. From any page, :book-open: at the top takes you back to the text you were reading.

## Language and Pāḷi script {#language}

There are two separate settings, both in the menu :ellipsis-vertical:

- **Pali script**: the letters the Pāḷi text is shown in. There are 22 scripts (Sinhala, Thai, Myanmar, Devanagari, Roman, Khmer and others).
- **Interface language**: the language of the buttons, menus and this help.

You can type searches in any script, whatever script you read in.

## Finding a text {#contents}

- :menu: **Contents** opens the list of all the books. The tabs at its top switch between the **Mūla** (the Tipiṭaka), **Aṭṭhakathā** (commentaries), **Ṭīkā** (sub-commentaries) and **Añña** (other texts). Tap a book to open its chapters.
- On wide screens the contents stay open beside the text.
- The [home page](/) lists the books, the texts you read last (**Continue reading**) and some suttas to start with. Open it from the menu :ellipsis-vertical: › :house: **Home**, or with the logo at the top left on wide screens.
- To find a sutta by its name, see [Searching](#search).
- The text scrolls on to the next chapter. **Show previous text** at the top shows what comes before.

## Word meanings {#dictionary}

- Tap a word in the text. The **Dictionary** panel shows the grammar of the word and its meaning in the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD) and the other dictionaries.
- On phones the panel is at the bottom of the screen: drag its handle to make it taller or shorter. :x: closes it.
- **More** opens the word on the full dictionary page.
- Choose the dictionaries in :ellipsis-vertical: › :settings: **More settings** › **Dictionaries**, for example the Thai, Sinhala or Myanmar dictionaries.
- To look up a word you type, use the **Dictionary** search (see [Searching](#search)).
- If you do not want the dictionary to open when you tap, turn off **Tap a word to look it up in the dictionary** in the settings.

## The paragraph menu :ellipsis: {#paragraph}

Every paragraph, verse and heading has an options button :ellipsis: in the left margin. With a mouse it appears when you point at the paragraph. On a touch screen, tap the paragraph once to show it, or tap the paragraph number. The menu has:

- :share-2: **Get a link**: copies a link to this paragraph, to share or to save.
- :copy: **Copy the paragraph**.
- :columns-2: **Open Aṭṭhakathā alongside** (or Ṭīkā, Mūla): opens the matching commentary next to the text. See [Commentary](#commentary).
- :panel-bottom-open: **Show the commentary here**: the commentary on this paragraph, in the panel.
- :map-pin: **Where was this taught?**: the place, the teacher and the people of the sutta, with a map.
- :star: **Add a bookmark**. Your bookmarks are on the [Bookmarks](/bookmarks) page, in the menu :ellipsis-vertical:.

## Commentary {#commentary}

- **Underlined words** are explained in the commentary. Tap one to read the explanation in the **Commentary** tab of the panel.
- :ellipsis: › :panel-bottom-open: **Show the commentary here** shows the whole commentary on a paragraph.
- :ellipsis: › :columns-2: **Open Aṭṭhakathā alongside** shows the commentary as a second column. The two columns scroll together, so the commentary stays next to the passage it explains.
- You can turn the underlines off in the settings (**Underline the words explained in the commentaries**).

## Searching {#search}

The search box at the top has three kinds of search. The button at the left of the box switches between them; on the results page they are tabs.

- :text-search: **Full text**: finds a word or phrase in all the texts. A word also finds its other endings (dhamma → dhammo, dhammassa…); **Exact word** finds only the word as typed. `*` stands for any letters: `*ssa` finds words ending in ssa. Choose the word form on the left, then a text, to see each place it occurs.
- :heading: **Titles**: finds a sutta, chapter or book by its name, for example `brahmajāla` or `metta`.
- :book-a: **Dictionary**: looks up a word in the dictionaries.

:list-filter: **Filter** limits the search to some collections, for example only the Mūla. :book-open: at the top takes you back to the text you were reading.

## Reading settings {#reading}

The colours and the text size are in the menu :ellipsis-vertical:. In :settings: **More settings** there are also:

- **Appearance**: line spacing and text width.
- **Variant readings**: the readings of other editions, as a marker (tap it to read), inline, or hidden.
- **Page numbers**: shows the page numbers of the Myanmar, VRI, PTS or Thai printed editions.
- **Paragraph numbers**.

## Tabs and columns {#tabs}

In :settings: **More settings** › **Tabs**:

- **Single**: a text you open replaces the current one. Best on phones.
- **Tabs**: every text opens in a new tab.
- **Columns**: the open texts are side by side. :layers: lists the open texts.

## Places and people {#places}

[Places and people](/names) lists the places, people and peoples of the texts, with the passages that mention them. The [Map](/map) shows the places, the regions (mahājanapadas) and the journeys of the Buddha. From a paragraph: :ellipsis: › :map-pin: **Where was this taught?**

## Apps and offline use {#apps}

The same app is in Google Play, the App Store, the Microsoft Store and the Mac App Store (links on the [home page](/) of the website). The apps work without an internet connection.
